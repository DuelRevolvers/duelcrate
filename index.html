❓ How to use DuelCrate

🎤 Hosting a show

  1. Create a Game — pick a game style, then fill in your questions. Everything autosaves as you type.
  2. Play a Game — choose your game and a room opens with a 4-letter room code and a QR code.
  3. Put your screen where everyone can see it (a TV works great), wait for players to appear in the lobby, then hit Start Game.
  4. Run the show from your screen: open buzzers, reveal answers, and judge with the ✓ / ✗ buttons. The bottom strip has music controls.
  5. Scores update live. Every game ends on a final scores screen — then close the room or host another.

📱 Playing along

  1. On your phone, scan the lobby's QR code or open the player join page.
  2. Enter your name and the 4-letter room code from the host's screen.
  3. Your phone becomes your controller — a buzzer, answer choices, or a place to type guesses and wagers, depending on the game.
  4. Watch the host's screen for the questions. Your phone shows your score and what to do next.
  5. If you get disconnected, just rejoin with the same name — your score will be waiting for you.

DuelCrate runs entirely in your browsers — the host's device is the game server, so keep it awake and online during the show.
